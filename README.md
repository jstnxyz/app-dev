# My Favorite Movie: The Conjuring

**The Conjuring** (2013) is a supernatural horror film directed by *James Wan*. It is the first installment in *The Conjuring Universe* and is based on the real-life case files of paranormal investigators **Ed and Lorraine Warren**.

> "Based on the true case files of the Warrens."

## Why I Love This Movie
- **Atmosphere and Tension:** Instead of relying only on cheap jump scares, the film builds genuine dread through clever camera work and sound design.
- **Compelling Characters:** Patrick Wilson and Vera Farmiga give grounded, heartfelt performances as Ed and Lorraine Warren.
- **Classic Haunted House Vibes:** The story of the Perron family in their secluded Rhode Island farmhouse feels like a timeless ghost story.
